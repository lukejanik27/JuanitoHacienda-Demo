Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take reservations?

Yes! Book online on our reservations page. Walk-ins are always welcome, and the patio is first come, first served.

Do you have vegetarian and gluten-free options?

Absolutely. Look for the V and GF badges on our menu, and let your server know about any allergies.

Is there parking?

There is a free lot behind the restaurant, plus street parking on Cactus Lane.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Call us at (555) 010-1234 and we'll have it ready in about 20 minutes.

Do you host kids' birthday parties?

We do! See our events page for private dining and party packages.
