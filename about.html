Our Story

From six tables and one stove to the neighborhood's favorite hacienda.

It Started With Abuela's Recipes

In 1987, Juan "Juanito" Morales traded a long career on the road for a tiny storefront on Cactus Lane. His wife Lucía brought the recipes she grew up with: the mole, the pozole, the salsas that took all morning.

Word spread one plate at a time. Neighbors became regulars, regulars became family, and the six-table cantina kept growing, without ever losing its heart.

🌶️

Our Journey

1987

Juanito and Lucía open the doors with six tables and a borrowed stove.

1994

We add our wood-fired grill and the carne asada becomes legend.

2003

The dining room expands, and the patio is built around an old cottonwood tree.

2012

Chef Rosa Morales takes over the kitchen and starts milling heirloom corn in-house.

2019

Launch of our catering team and private dining room.

2026

Still family-owned, still making tortillas every morning.
